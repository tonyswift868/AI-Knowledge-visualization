# Third-party 2D teacher assets

The Shizuku prototype is copied from the official DragonBonesJS Pixi demo.
It is distributed under the MIT license. See the adjacent `LICENSE` and
`SOURCE.md` files in the asset directory.

This is a replaceable prototype asset, not MentorFlow's final original
teacher artwork. Production character replacement only needs a new manifest
and the same `Teacher2DRenderer` adapter contract.
